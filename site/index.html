About me

My officiating log starts at the Utah Olympic Oval in October 2020. Since then, I’ve worked youth, high school, junior, college, adult, and sled hockey, including games in the NA3HL, NCDC, and ACHA Divisions I and II.

I returned to the Rocky Mountain District Advanced Officials Experience as an Instructor Intern in 2025 after attending in 2022, 2023, and 2024.
